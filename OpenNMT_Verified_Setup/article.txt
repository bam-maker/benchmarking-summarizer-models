london -lrb- reuters -rrb- -- a city council has approved a plan to replace 200 diesel buses with electric vehicles over the next three years . the first 50 buses will begin operating in september , officials said on tuesday . the project will cost 80 million pounds and will be funded by the council and a national transport grant . transport director sarah james said the new vehicles would reduce air pollution and lower maintenance costs . charging stations will be installed at two bus depots before the first vehicles arrive . existing routes and ticket prices will remain unchanged . the council said it would publish progress reports every six months .
